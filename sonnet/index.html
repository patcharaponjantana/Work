Game engine
Unreal's IK Retargeter runs all five stages in the editor — pose matching, chain definition, FK retarget, IK post-pass — and bakes or previews at runtime.
Film / VFX
MotionBuilder's "Characterize" step is the bind-pose alignment stage. Its Story solver runs retarget + IK pass simultaneously on the timeline.
Real-time avatar
Body tracking drivers (VR, phone) emit a normalized skeleton. The pipeline adapts that signal to custom avatar proportions — all five stages, every frame.

A T-pose source bind = 0°. An A-pose target bind ≈ 30°. The naive approach copies the source's final angle; the corrected approach applies the animation delta from the target's own bind — producing the same motion magnitude regardless of bind difference.

Bottom-heavy weighting concentrates bend in the lumbar region — matches human anatomy for forward bends. Top-heavy concentrates in the thoracic — useful for creature rigs with long necks. Even is the neutral default that preserves shape most faithfully when transitioning between rigs of similar size.
